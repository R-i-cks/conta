Assumes monthly amortising payments and constant TAN until payoff, including the current rate phase of a mixed-rate contract. No balloon, grace period, leasing or revolving credit. Portuguese rates, clearly separated

TAN is the nominal annual interest rate used to calculate interest and instalments. TAEG is the lender’s annual total-cost rate; it is shown for reference and is not added to TAN. Enter insurance, account fees and other loan-only monthly costs separately to include them in the estimate.

2026 fee defaults: home loans up to 0.5% at a variable rate or 2% at a fixed rate. Consumer loans contracted from July 2009: fixed rate up to 0.5% with more than a year remaining, or 0.25% with a year or less; variable rate 0%. The consumer commission is also capped by remaining fixed-period interest. Lower agreed rates and exemptions may apply. Fees are charged only on extra principal and capped at the applicable maximum. The temporary mortgage fee suspension ended on 31 December 2025.
